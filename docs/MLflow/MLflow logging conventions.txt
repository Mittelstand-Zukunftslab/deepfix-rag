rag_request contract:
- `rag_request` is the mandatory semantic root span for one Target RAG request.
- It starts before request-specific RAG processing begins and ends after the final RAG output has been produced.
- All applicable contract stages must be descendants of `rag_request`.
- Use the native `rag_request` span duration as the authoritative end-to-end RAG execution latency.
- Do not add a duplicate custom total-latency field.
- Automatic framework/provider spans may appear beneath the applicable contract stage spans, but they do not replace `rag_request` or the required semantic stage spans.
